Norwegian fjords are among the most impressive natural wonders in Europe.
They were shaped by glaciers and are deep sea inlets surrounded by steep mountains.

The best known are Geirangerfjord and Nærøyfjord, both listed as UNESCO World Heritage sites.
In summer, the fjords attract visitors with cruises, kayaking, and scenic hiking trails.

These regions combine silence, clean air, and dramatic landscapes,
showing one of the wildest and most beautiful sides of Norway.
