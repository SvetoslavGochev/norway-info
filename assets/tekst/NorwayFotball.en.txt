Football in Norway has grown steadily over the last decades, both at club and national team level.
The domestic league Eliteserien is known for high intensity, youth development, and strong local fan culture.

One of the biggest milestones in recent years is the rise of a new generation of players,
including stars such as Erling Haaland and Martin Odegaard, who increased international interest in Norwegian football.

Historically, Norway had major moments in the 1990s, qualifying for FIFA World Cups and becoming a difficult opponent in Europe.
Today, the focus is on long-term development, modern academies, and improving competitiveness in UEFA tournaments.

If you visit Norway, attending a live match is a great way to experience local atmosphere, chants, and football culture up close.
